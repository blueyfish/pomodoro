<p>This is a very simple and tiny pomodoro timer app for Android 2.3 and later, with support for WearOS devices too!</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>Countdown & countup timer (up to 96 hours)</li><li>Cyan flashing and/or alarm sounds reminder when each pomodori completed</li><li>Normal texts or text arts display</li></ul><p><b>Controls:</b></p><ul><li><b>Single tap:</b> Pause & resume active timer, Dismiss flashing reminder</li><li><b>Double tap:</b> Stop active timer</li><li><b>Long tap:</b> Set a new timer</li><li><b>Swiping:</b> Switch between text art fonts</li></ul>
